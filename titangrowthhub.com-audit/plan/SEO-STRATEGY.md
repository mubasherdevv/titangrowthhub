# Enterprise SEO Strategy: titangrowthhub.com
**Client:** Titan Growth Hub  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Vertical:** SEO & Digital Marketing Agency  

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary
As a digital marketing and SEO agency, Titan Growth Hub must demonstrate absolute excellence on its own web property. By correcting original design/migration issues (such as branding inconsistencies and duplicate canonicals) and expanding schema infrastructure, the agency will target high-intent B2B search terms.

---

## 📈 Search Architecture & Pillars
1. **Core Service Columns:** Web Design/Development, SEO, PPC, and Brand Strategy.
2. **Authority Pillars (Insights):** Thought leadership on search algorithmic updates, CRO case reports, and Next.js development performance.
3. **E-E-A-T Assets:** Proof of results through structured Case Studies and complete Employee entities.

---

## 🏢 Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Strategy
To capture market share in AI search engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity):
- **Citable Statistics:** Publish original surveys, data, and case study outcomes so AI models use Titan Growth Hub as a citation source.
- **llms.txt Directory:** Implement a plain-text B2B summary catalog for crawler parsing.
- **Entity Linking:** Link the `Person` schema of authors to their LinkedIn/external publications to build entity authority.
